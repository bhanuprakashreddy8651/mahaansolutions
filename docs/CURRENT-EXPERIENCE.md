# Current portfolio experience

The active design has removed CartoonFounder and FounderPuppet from the page. No body deformation, synthetic blinking, walking, or speaking face is rendered. The founder appears once in an editorial portrait using the supplied original photograph. The image is cropped and resized without retouching the face.

ServiceJourney provides three interactive stages for each confirmed service. Visitors can select a stage or advance through the approach. Its small transitions honor pause and reduced-motion preferences. The closing has a direct contact invitation instead of another founder character.

The light ivory/navy/gold palette is retained. Optional English browser narration is explicitly a guide, not a recording of the founder. Realistic Flow footage can be reviewed and integrated later; none is implied to exist.

Validation: scripts/editorial-qa.cjs checks the absence of puppet canvases and avatar anchors, a single founder portrait, journey interaction, service switching/filtering, contact finale, mobile overflow and reduced motion. It supersedes puppet-qa.cjs, entrance-qa.cjs, founder-qa.cjs and company-qa.cjs for the current design. Screenshots: docs/editorial-hero.png, editorial-services.png, editorial-closing.png and editorial-mobile.png.

The existing enquiry limitations remain: the hosted static form prepares a brief, and configured server credentials are required for database submission. Direct email, phone and WhatsApp remain available.
